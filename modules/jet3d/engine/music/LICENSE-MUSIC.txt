Music (FM synthesis) — third-party code and licensing
=====================================================

Everything in this directory except compat/, jet_music.c, jet_music.h and
genmidi_bank.h is carried in from other projects, unmodified except where a
change is tagged MESHPUNK. It reached jet3d by way of the meshpunk doom
module, which uses the same stack.

  dbopl.c, dbopl.h

      r3635.  Copyright (C) 2002-2010  The DOSBox Team.
      GNU General Public License, version 2 or (at your option) any later.

  i_oplmusic.c, opl.h, opl_queue.c, opl_queue.h, midifile.c, midifile.h
      The OPL music player and MIDI parser from Chocolate Doom.
      Copyright (C) 1993-1996 Id Software, Inc.
      Copyright (C) 2005-2014 Simon Howard.
      GNU General Public License, version 2 or (at your option) any later.

  memio.c, memio.h, mus2mid.c, mus2mid.h
      Memory-backed IO and the MUS-to-MIDI converter, also Chocolate Doom,
      same copyright and licence as above.

  opl_tdeck.c, opl_tdeck.h
      Written for meshpunk. The OPL backend: runs the emulated chip at the
      mixer's rate and dispatches the sequencer's timer callbacks.

Compatibility with jet3d's licence
----------------------------------
jet3d is AGPL because of the Jet rasteriser (see ../../JET-LICENSE.txt).
Every carried-in file above is "GPL version 2 or any later version", which
may be used as GPLv3, and AGPLv3 section 13 expressly permits combining
AGPLv3 work with GPLv3 work. The combined module is therefore distributable,
and stays copyleft -- which jet3d already was.

Instruments
-----------
The FM instrument bank (genmidi_bank.h) is NOT Doom's GENMIDI lump, which is
proprietary. It is generated by tools/make_genmidi.py, authored for this
game, and carries no third-party rights.
